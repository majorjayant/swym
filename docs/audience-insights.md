Version 0.1, 2026-11-17. Author: Audience & Psychology Analyst agent. Inputs: BRD, PRD, content model, competitor research brief (19 sites), feature_matrix.csv, search_visibility.md, deliverables/BACKGROUND_RESEARCH.md.

Everything here is a **hypothesis to test** with real visitors, not a fact about any group. Rows describe situations and needs, never traits. Statistics are from the competitor feature matrix (19 sites; "not found" counted as no, so percentages are floors) unless another source is named. Items marked `[HYPOTHESIS]` have no source yet.

## 0. The one thing all audiences share

Used-car dealers start with a trust deficit. In Gallup's 2023 honesty and ethics poll, about 8% of US adults rated car salespeople high or very high for honesty (Gallup, via BACKGROUND_RESEARCH.md; US data, no Canadian equivalent checked). The job of every page is therefore to remove reasons to doubt before asking for anything. Saskatchewan law helps: an advertised price must be the drive-away price (all dealer fees except PST/GST) with a stock number, ads must show the dealer's name or DL number, and used vehicles under 200,000 km carry a minimum 30-day / 1,000 km powertrain warranty (FCAA sources, BRD C7). Only 3 of 19 competitor sites show a licence number and none disclosed a doc fee amount (research brief s. 1, s. 3). Saying these things plainly is a differentiator, not a legal burden.

## 4. Objection-handling FAQ (questions only; answers by the copywriter)

Owner input needed: **Y** = answer needs facts from the owner (`needsOwnerInput: true`); **N** = answer can be written from sources now.

### Buying
1. What does "drive-away price" mean? (N: SK rule; price includes all dealer fees except PST/GST; stock number shown)
2. Can I take a test drive? (Y: policy, ID needed, booking in Phase 2)
3. Do you inspect your vehicles before sale? (Y: checklist; no claim until supplied)
4. Is there a warranty? (Y: terms; may cite SK minimum 30 days / 1,000 km under 200,000 km with lawyer marker)
5. Can I bring my own mechanic or get the vehicle checked? (Y)

### Pricing and fees
6. Are there documentation or admin fees on top of the price? (N for principle; Y for whether any fee exists when no price is advertised)
7. Do I pay a deposit to hold a vehicle, and is it refundable? (Y; SK caps deposits at 2% of list price before a contract, cite with lawyer marker)
8. What taxes apply in Saskatchewan? (N: PST and GST are excluded from drive-away price; rates themselves `[PLACEHOLDER]` unless sourced)

### Financing and credit
9. Can I get financing with no credit history or bad credit? (Y: no approval promise; "we work with lenders" only if true)
10. Will checking my options affect my credit score? (Y: soft check only if offered; Phase 2)
11. What do lenders look at? (N: general, no rates)
12. What documents do I need for financing? (Y)

### Selling and trade-in
13. How do you value my car? (Y)
14. How long is your offer good for, and how fast am I paid? (Y: the promises on the sell band)
15. I still owe money on my car. Can I sell or trade it? (Y: lien handling)
16. Do you offer consignment? (Y: owner must say yes or no)

### Newcomers and documents
17. I'm new to Canada. Can I buy a car here? (Y: documents accepted; no approval claim)
18. What do I need to register and insure a car in Saskatchewan? (N for the fact that SGI handles registration and insurance `[HYPOTHESIS: confirm current process]`; Y for anything the dealer does on the buyer's behalf)
19. Can I bring a family member or friend to help? (N: yes, plainly)

### Visiting us
20. Where are you and when are you open? (Y: hours; address known)
21. Can I reach you by text or WhatsApp? (Y: which numbers)

## 5. Language toggle

Recommendation: **not in Phase 0; build it in Phase 3, professionally translated**, Punjabi first, then Hindi; French only if a source shows demand.

- Evidence is thin: only Siman offers a French phone line and 11% of the 19 sites have any other language (feature matrix). No site in the study offers Punjabi or Hindi, so it would be a differentiator, but there is no demand data yet.
- Risk of doing it badly is higher than of not doing it: a machine-translated page about money and contracts can mislead. If translated, it must be by a professional, reviewed by a fluent speaker who knows SK vehicle terms, and kept in sync by the CMS.
- CMS support now: every text field is a `LocalizedString { en, pa?, hi?, fr? }` (content model s. 2), and `enabledLocales` controls the toggle. Nothing to rebuild later.
- Interim, Phase 0–2: a plain sentence in English on Contact and in FAQ 19 saying callers are welcome to bring someone to translate, and `[PLACEHOLDER: languages spoken by staff]` if the owner confirms any. This serves A4 without a toggle.
- Validate before Phase 3: browser language of visitors (analytics, with consent), FAQ 17–19 expansion rates, and asking walk-in customers.

## 6. Tone notes for the copywriter

- Calm, straight, sure (Guidelines v1.0 p. 18–19). Short sentences. One idea per sentence.
- Name the worry, then answer it. "Will the price change at the desk? No. The price you see is the drive-away price, plus GST and PST."
- No urgency, no exclamation marks, no "guaranteed", "lowest", "best deals", "free", "savings" (FCAA wording limits, BRD C7).
- Say "you" and "we". Avoid "customers" and "clients".
- Canadian spelling: kilometres, colour, licence (noun), cheque, centre.
- Never describe a group; describe a situation ("if you've just moved to Canada", "if your credit took a hit").
- Every number or promise is a placeholder until the owner supplies it.
