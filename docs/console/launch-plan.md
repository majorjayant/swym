# Owner console: 30-day launch plan

## Day 1 (site goes live)
- Connect: the site beacon (lead actions, visits, vehicle views) and Google Search Console (read-only).
- Enter once: date acquired, purchase cost and reconditioning for each car on the lot; this month's expenses; the Google rating and review count.
- Tag the Google Business Profile website link with `utm_source=gbp`.
- No ads. No GA4 or Clarity.

## Days 1–30: observe (about 10 minutes a week)
- Record each sale on the day it happens (Finance → Record a sale).
- Enter walk-ins and referrals, and update the Google rating once a week.
- Monday check on the Overview: the five tiles and "Needs attention". Act on aged cars and on vehicles with views but no leads.
- Note new search-term opportunities on the Marketing page. Don't act on them yet.

## Day 30: baseline review
Freeze five numbers (Growth page): leads per week, lead to sale, cars sold, gross per car, new reviews and rating.

Go / no-go for ads:
- Lead to sale is at least 10%, and every sale has its gross recorded → start ads.
- Otherwise, fix the site and pricing first and review again in 2 weeks.

## After day 30, if go
1. **Google Search ads first.** Small budget (about $300–500 for 30 days), aimed at the opportunity terms (for example "used cars regina", "used trucks regina").
2. **Meta second,** and only if Google's cost per sale stays under about 15% of gross per car.
3. Every ad link carries `utm_source` = google or meta, `utm_medium` = cpc or paid_social, `utm_campaign` = year-month-theme, and `utm_content` = stock number for single-car ads.
4. Connect the Google Ads API and Meta Marketing API, read-only, for daily spend and clicks. The "Not running" panels then fill in.
5. Add only if needed:
   - **GA4**, for cross-device or audience reports, or Google Ads conversion import.
   - **Microsoft Clarity**, if a car keeps getting views and no leads and recordings would show why.
